02

The winning formula

Shared by all five accounts — mostly missing from ours.

1

Their own voice

Voiceover on 100% of posts. We ride trending sounds — the single biggest gap. Your voice is the brand.

2

A side taken

Never "X happened." Always a loud opinion. Adika's "I'm jealous Barça get stars for free" hit 709K.

3

A persona

bi_benard is a character ("Coach B.I") and earns the highest engagement rate here on personality alone.

4

Debate bait

Messi/Ronaldo & club rivalry = comment wars. tomide built 174K almost entirely on it.

5

Speed on the moment

Our two biggest hits were a live half-time reaction (14.1K) and a hot take — post news same-hour.

6

Named series + funnel

Recurring playlists (KOMBO) + off-platform community, then monetise once big (Adika, berneese).

03

Us vs them: the fix

Same information — the framing is what separates 200 likes from 100K.

Before · ~200 likes

"Real Madrid is gearing up for a massive season."

After · the pattern that pops

"Real Madrid are NOT winning the league and I'll die on this hill. Their midfield's a mess. Tell me I'm wrong 👇"

Before · audio

Trending sound + on-screen text, no voice.

After · audio

Your face, your voice, original audio — every core post.

Before · cadence

3–10 posts/day, neutral, no persona.

After · cadence

3–5 sharp posts/day, 1 daily "swing", consistent Barça persona.
