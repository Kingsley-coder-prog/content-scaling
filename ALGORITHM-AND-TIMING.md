## 2. How the algorithm ranks you

TikTok shows a new video to a small test batch (~200–500 people), reads the reaction, and promotes it to bigger batches if signals are strong. Signals by weight:

1. **Watch time / completion rate** 🥇 — the biggest factor.
2. **Rewatches / loops**
3. **Shares** — strongest engagement signal.
4. **Comments**
5. **Saves**, then **Likes**
6. **Follows from the video**

Everything below is just how to win those six.

---

## 3. The levers (do these every post)

**Retention — most important:**
- Hook in **1–2 seconds**; say the most divisive thing first, on camera. No intros.
- Keep takes **12–25s**; shorter = easier high completion %. Only go longer if every second earns it.
- **Build a loop** — end on a line that flows back into the first line so it replays. Rewatches are rocket fuel.
- **On-screen captions always** (most first views are on mute).

**Engagement:**
- **Reply to every comment in the first 30–60 min** — cheapest boost there is. Turn spicy comments into your next "reply" video.
- **Bait shares & saves by name** — "send this to a Madrid fan," "save this XI for matchday." Shares > likes.
- End every post with **one clear question** (pick a fight).

**Discovery / TikTok search (underrated):**
- TikTok is a search engine. **Say keywords out loud** (auto-captions index them) and put them in on-screen text + caption: "Barcelona," "Yamal," "La Liga."
- Use **Creator Search Insights** in-app to find what football fans search but nobody answers — then make that video.

**Consistency (trains the algorithm):**
- Stay in the **Barça/football** lane — every on-niche post teaches TikTok who to serve you to.
- Post daily; the account gets classified and targeted faster.

## 4. Reaching USA / UK / international English speakers

The For You feed is **global and content-based, not nationality-based** — it serves whoever it predicts will engage, anywhere.

- **Language:** create in English (on-screen text + spoken keywords) so you're tagged as English content.
- **Topic:** Barça is a global brand — your biggest advantage. Champions League, Yamal, Messi, GOAT debates, big transfers travel worldwide.
- **Schedule for US/UK prime:** use TikTok's scheduler to drop a post ~**1–2 AM WAT (= 7–8 PM US Eastern)** while you sleep.
- **Keep pidgin/local slang light on global posts** — great for the Nigerian core (bi_benard proves it), but heavy slang caps international shares. Pidgin for community/banter; clean English for the big global takes.
- **Reply to non-Nigerian commenters** — signals the algorithm to keep serving those regions.
- Prefer **globally-relevant angles** (UCL, best XI, GOAT war) over hyper-local Nigerian-football references when you want reach.

## 5. Reach-killers to avoid

- ❌ Watermarked / reposted content (CapCut is fine; other-app or reposted TikTok-logo clips get suppressed)
- ❌ Deleting and re-uploading the same video
- ❌ 15+ hashtags or spammy/banned ones — keep the tight 4–5 mix
- ❌ External-link / "follow my other page" spam early on
- ❌ Jumping niches or long gaps between posts
