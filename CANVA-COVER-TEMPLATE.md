# Reusable Canva Cover Template — @ultimatepichichi

Build this **once** in Canva, then every day you just swap the player photo + drop in the headline/subhead your routine gives you (the "🎨 GRAPHIC BRIEF"). Goal: a recognisable, pro transfer-graphic look — like the announcement edits — in ~2 minutes per cover.

---

## 1. Canvas & safe zones

- **Size:** **1080 × 1920 px** (9:16). In Canva: *Create design → Custom size → 1080 × 1920*. This is the TikTok video-cover size.
- **Why it matters:** TikTok crops covers differently in two places, so keep all vital stuff in the middle:
  - **Top safe margin:** 100 px (leave clear).
  - **Bottom UI zone:** keep critical text/faces **above y ≈ 1580** (the caption + username overlay the bottom in-feed, and the grid crops it).
  - **Right buttons zone:** keep text **left of x ≈ 950** (like/comment/share sit on the right in-feed).
- **Design rule:** headline in the **top third**, player cut-out fills the **centre**, small details near the bottom bar.

---

## 2. Brand kit (set this up once)

In Canva: *Brand → Brand Kit* → add these colours and fonts so they're one click every day.

**Colours (hex):**
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blaugrana garnet | `#A50044` | primary accent, key words, brush strokes |
| Barça blue | `#004D98` | secondary accent, gradients |
| Gold | `#EDBB00` | player name / "DONE DEAL"-style highlight |
| Deep navy (bg) | `#0B1220` | background base, scrims |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | main headline text |
| Light grey | `#C7CBD6` | small detail/sub text |

**Fonts (all free in Canva):**
- **Headline:** **Anton** (or **Bebas Neue**) — heavy, condensed, UPPERCASE, tight letter-spacing.
- **Accent word** (the punchy bit, e.g. "HOT TAKE", "DONE DEAL"): **Permanent Marker** or **Archivo Black Italic**, in garnet or gold.
- **Details / subhead:** **Montserrat** SemiBold/Bold, UPPERCASE, +40 letter-spacing.
- Keep these **identical every day** — consistency is what makes covers instantly "yours."

---

## 3. Layer stack (bottom → top)

1. **Background** — a dark, dramatic stadium photo (Camp Nou / generic floodlit stadium). Desaturate it.
2. **Colour wash** — a rectangle with a **garnet→blue diagonal gradient**, set to *Overlay* or ~35% opacity, for the blaugrana mood.
3. **Top scrim** — a black→transparent gradient rectangle across the top ~40% (so white headline is always readable over any photo).
4. **Player cut-out (PNG, no background)** — the day's subject, large, centred/slightly right. Head near **y≈600**, feet near **y≈1650**.
5. **Secondary faded figure (optional)** — a second player, lower opacity, behind, for depth (like the example's ghosted action shot).
6. **Headline stack** (top third) — 2–3 lines (see §4).
7. **Series badge** — a small pill top-left with the day's series name.
8. **Bottom detail bar (optional)** — thin bar ~y1500–1650 with the subhead + a small crest/flag.
9. **Watermark** — `@ultimatepichichi` small, top-right or centred ~y1500, 70% white.

---

## 4. Text layout (positions on the 1080×1920 canvas)

- **Series badge** — top-left, x≈60, y≈120. Rounded rectangle in garnet, white Montserrat Bold ~34pt, e.g. `CULÉ HOT TAKE`.
- **Watermark handle** — top-right, y≈130, Montserrat 30pt, white 70%: `@ultimatepichichi`.
- **HEADLINE stack** — centred, within x-margins 70–1000, starting y≈200:
  - **Line 1 — subject:** Anton ~150pt, **WHITE** (e.g. `LAMINE YAMAL`).
  - **Line 2 — the verdict/hook:** Anton ~100pt, WHITE with the **key word in garnet `#A50044` or gold `#EDBB00`** (e.g. `IS ALREADY OUR BEST PLAYER`).
  - **Line 3 — accent (optional):** Permanent Marker ~90pt, gold, slight tilt (e.g. `FIGHT ME 🔥`).
- **Bottom detail bar** — y≈1520, Montserrat SemiBold 40pt, light grey, one line (e.g. `19 YEARS OLD · WORLD CHAMPION`).

Add a subtle **drop shadow** or dark stroke on all white text so it never disappears into the photo.

---

## 5. Daily reuse workflow (~2 min)

1. Open the template → **duplicate the page** (keep the master clean).
2. **Swap the player photo:** upload the day's player, remove its background (Canva *Edit image → BG Remover*, or free: remove.bg / PhotoRoom), drop it into the cut-out slot.
3. **Change the background** stadium only if the vibe needs it (usually leave it).
4. **Paste the headline + subhead** from the routine's `🎨 GRAPHIC BRIEF` into the 3 text lines. Colour the key word garnet or gold.
5. **Update the series badge** label (Transfer Truth / Hot Take / etc.).
6. **Download** as PNG → set it as your TikTok cover when uploading.

---

## 6. Per-post-type tweaks (same template, small swaps)

Keep the layout; change only the **badge label** and the **accent colour** so each pillar has a subtle identity:

| Post type | Badge | Accent colour | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer Truth | `TRANSFER TRUTH` | gold `#EDBB00` | crest + flag, "DONE DEAL / BIN IT" |
| Hot Take | `CULÉ HOT TAKE 🔥` | garnet `#A50044` | big bold claim, single player |
| Blaugrana Verdict | `RATED` | blue `#004D98` | player + a big number/score |
| Legends/Emotion | `CULÉ CONFESSIONS` | gold, softer scrim | warm, nostalgic photo |
| Rival / GOAT War | `VS` | split garnet/rival colour | two players facing off |

For rival banter, swap the wash to a **split** (blaugrana one side, rival colour the other) — reuse the same text layout.

---

## 7. Do / don't

- ✅ Same fonts, same headline position, same badge style **every day** → instantly recognisable in the feed.
- ✅ High contrast: white headline + dark scrim. If you can't read it in 0.5s, fix it.
- ✅ Real player photos + real crests go **in Canva** (not AI) — that's what gives the polished look.
- ❌ Don't crowd the bottom-right (TikTok UI covers it).
- ❌ Don't change the layout per post — only the photo, text, badge, and accent colour.
- ❌ Don't use tiny text — headline should be readable as a thumbnail.

> Pair this with the `🎨 COVER IMAGE` brief in each daily post from the routine, and see [TEMPLATES.md](TEMPLATES.md) §8. The AI image prompt is for stylised backgrounds; this Canva template is for the polished announcement covers.
