Fill in the `[brackets]`, hit record, post. Every script assumes **on camera + your own voice**.

## 1. Voiceover / on-camera script templates

Structure for all: **HOOK (0–2s) → TAKE → EVIDENCE → DEBATE-BAIT → SIGN-OFF.** Keep to 15–30s.

### A. Hot Take
> **Hook:** "Hot take — [player/team] is [bold claim], and I'll die on this hill."
> **Take:** "Everyone's saying [popular opinion]. That's wrong."
> **Evidence:** "Because [1 sharp reason / 1 stat]."
> **Debate-bait:** "Tell me why I'm wrong. I'll wait."
> **Sign-off:** "[Your catchphrase]."

### B. Match Reaction (Barça Watch)
> **Hook:** "We're [X] minutes in and I already have thoughts."
> **Take:** "[Player] was [rating]/10 and it's not close."
> **Evidence:** "[One thing he did / didn't do]."
> **Debate-bait:** "Who was YOUR man of the match? Because it wasn't [obvious pick]."
> **Sign-off:** "[Catchphrase]."

### C. Transfer Truth (rumour rating)
> **Hook:** "This Barça rumour is [a lie / actually happening]."
> **Take:** "I'm rating it: [Believe it / Bin it]."
> **Evidence:** "Here's the one thing everyone's missing: [reason]."
> **Debate-bait:** "Believe it or bin it? Go."
> **Sign-off:** "[Catchphrase]."

### D. Culé Classroom (analyst breakdown — your edge)
> **Hook:** "Nobody explains [concept] properly, so I will — in 30 seconds."
> **Take:** "[The thing] works because [core idea]."
> **Evidence:** "Watch [player/moment] here — [what to notice]."
> **Debate-bait:** "Now you'll see it every game. Save this."
> **Sign-off:** "[Catchphrase]."

### E. Ranking / XI (Blaugrana Verdict)
> **Hook:** "I'm ranking [X]. Number [N] will start a war."
> **Take:** run the list fast, strongest opinion on the controversial one.
> **Debate-bait:** "Screenshot it and tell me what I got wrong."
> **Sign-off:** "[Catchphrase]."

### F. GOAT War / Rival Banter
> **Hook:** "[Rival] fans are not going to survive this one."
> **Take:** "[Claim, said with a smirk]."
> **Evidence:** "[Stat / trophy / moment]."
> **Debate-bait:** "[Rival] fans, defend this in the comments."
> **Sign-off:** "[Catchphrase]."

### G. Reply-to-Comment (Culé Confessions)
> **Hook:** "You said THIS in my comments and you're dead wrong." (show the comment)
> **Take → Evidence → Debate-bait → Sign-off** as above.

## 6. CTA rotation (don't repeat the same one back-to-back)

- Comment: "Tell me why I'm wrong 👇" / "Believe or bin? 👇" / "Your XI in the comments."
- Follow: "Follow for daily Barça takes." / "Follow if you're a real Culé."
- Save: "Save this for matchday." / "Screenshot and come back in May."
- Share: "Send this to the Madrid fan in your life."

## 7. Daily quick-fire workflow

1. Pick today's series from the calendar (or react to live news).
2. Choose a hook from §2.
3. Fill a script template from §1 → record on camera, your voice.
4. Add on-screen text (§4) + write caption (§3).
5. Tight hashtags, own audio, post. End with a CTA (§6).
6. Reply to early comments in the first 30 min (feeds the algorithm + builds community).

## 8. Cover image per post

Every post needs a scroll-stopping cover. Produce two things:

**(i) AI image prompt** — for a stylised cover/background (paste into your image tool). Keep it generic — AI garbles real faces, crests, and text:
> "Dramatic football stadium at night, blaugrana deep-blue and garnet-red lighting, a lone silhouetted player, intense cinematic mood, bold empty space at top for a headline, high contrast, poster style."

**(ii) Graphic brief** — for a Canva/Photoshop announcement edit (the polished transfer-graphic look like real edits):
- **Headline** (≤6 words, e.g. "ADEYEMI — DONE DEAL")
- **Subhead** (e.g. "€29m from Dortmund")
- **Player to cut out** + **club**
- **Colour scheme** (blaugrana red/blue + accent)

Use real player photos + club crests *in the template* (Canva), not in AI generation. Keep the same font/layout every time so your covers are instantly recognisable as yours.

**Build the reusable Canva template once** using the full spec (size, safe zones, hex codes, fonts, layer-by-layer build, 2-min daily reuse): see [CANVA-COVER-TEMPLATE.md](CANVA-COVER-TEMPLATE.md).
